Profile

An engineer who connects the whole problem.

My core discipline is gas-turbine engine performance. I work across thermodynamic cycle design, component matching, off-design operability, controls and diagnostics, with model–test correlation connecting the analysis to the machine.

My path has taken me from UAV aerodynamics at XAG, through aero-engine modelling and diagnostics at Cranfield, to wireless sensing and experimental measurement at Politecnico di Milano. At Siemens Energy, that experience now supports my role as AI Consultant for Engineering and primary lead for AI4Engineering, following my move from Core Engineering & Development to AI & Enterprise in September 2026.

I turn engineering questions into requirements, data workflows, numerical and AI methods, and software that engineers can inspect and use. Current development spans industrial image analysis, measurement design and multi-view 3D workflows. I combine hands-on technical development with proof-of-concept leadership, university collaboration and industrial PhD supervision.

My focus is simple: understand the physics, measure the system, learn from the data, and turn that knowledge into better engineering decisions.

Decision Framework

How I turn evidence into engineering decisions.

A traceable route from a clearly defined engineering question to a qualified, deployable and reviewable decision.

  1. Define the question

    Set the physics, operating conditions, constraints, decision and acceptance criteria.

  2. Qualify the evidence

    Assess calibration, test conditions, traceability, representativeness and uncertainty.

  3. Validate the model

    Test the model and its interpretation against qualified evidence, limits and relevant failure modes.

  4. Decide, deploy and monitor

    Translate the result into a decision, tool or workflow with stated uncertainty and monitoring.

Two independent engineering checks

Evidence quality Is the evidence fit for this decision?

Check calibration, traceability, representativeness and uncertainty.

Model validity Is the model supported by qualified evidence?

Check correlation, limits, interpretation and relevant failure modes.

If either check fails, return to the relevant upstream stage before deployment.
